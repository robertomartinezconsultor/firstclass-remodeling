Biggest Cost Drivers in San Antonio Kitchen Remodels

Cabinets (30–40% of total budget)

Stock cabinets: $3,000–$8,000. Semi-custom: $8,000–$20,000. Full custom: $20,000–$45,000+. This is where the biggest budget decisions happen.

Countertops (10–20% of total budget)

Laminate: $1,500–$3,000. Granite: $3,000–$7,000. Quartz: $4,000–$9,000. Marble or quartzite: $6,000–$15,000+. Quartz is the most popular choice in San Antonio right now.

Layout Changes

Keeping the same layout saves $5,000–$15,000. Opening up a wall, moving the sink, or adding an island with plumbing requires additional electrical, plumbing, and structural work.

Appliances

Entry-level appliance package: $3,000–$6,000. Mid-range (Samsung, LG, KitchenAid): $6,000–$12,000. Professional (Wolf, Sub-Zero, Thermador): $15,000–$30,000+. We install but don't supply appliances unless requested.

Is a Kitchen Remodel Worth It in San Antonio?

A mid-range kitchen remodel in San Antonio typically returns 60–80% of its cost in added home value. In higher-value neighborhoods like Stone Oak, Boerne, and Shavano Park, returns can approach 85–90% on well-executed projects.

Beyond resale value, a new kitchen dramatically improves daily quality of life — and in San Antonio's competitive real estate market, updated kitchens help homes sell significantly faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in San Antonio TX?

A minor kitchen refresh costs $8,000–$15,000. A mid-range remodel runs $15,000–$35,000. A full high-end kitchen renovation with custom cabinets, quartz countertops, and new appliances can range from $35,000–$80,000+.

How long does a kitchen remodel take in San Antonio?

A minor update takes 1–2 weeks. A full kitchen remodel typically takes 3–6 weeks, depending on cabinet lead times, countertop fabrication, and the scope of plumbing or electrical work.

Yes. Kitchen remodels consistently offer strong ROI in San Antonio — typically 60–80% return, and higher in premium neighborhoods. Updated kitchens also help homes sell significantly faster in the current market.
