Smart Kitchen Upgrades in Texas (2026)

Which projects actually add value in Texas homes — and which ones to skip.

Not every kitchen upgrade delivers the same return. After working on kitchens across San Antonio, Helotes, Boerne, and surrounding Texas communities, here's our honest take on what's worth the investment in 2026.

High ROI Kitchen Upgrades in Texas

Cabinet Refacing or Replacement

If your cabinet boxes are solid, refacing (new doors, drawer fronts, hardware) costs $4,000–$9,000 and transforms the look completely. Full replacement runs more but is worth it when cabinets are damaged or outdated in style.

Countertop Upgrade to Quartz

Quartz is the most popular countertop in Texas right now — durable, low maintenance, and visually consistent. A quartz countertop upgrade for a typical San Antonio kitchen runs $3,500–$8,000 installed.

Open Concept Conversion

Removing a non-load-bearing wall between kitchen and living area is one of the highest-impact projects we do. Cost: $3,000–$8,000 depending on whether plumbing/electrical runs through the wall. ROI is exceptional.

Kitchen Island Addition

A freestanding island adds prep space and visual appeal without plumbing for $2,000–$5,000. A built-in island with seating runs $5,000–$15,000. With plumbing and electrical, budget $10,000–$20,000.

Upgrades That Don't Pay Off in Texas

  • High-end appliances in mid-range homes — A $10,000 range in a $250K home won't return its cost
  • Trendy tile patterns — Intricate backsplash designs date quickly and cost more to install and replace
  • Pot filler faucets — Popular on Instagram, rarely used in daily life, adds plumbing cost without ROI
