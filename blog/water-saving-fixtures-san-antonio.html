Water-Saving Fixtures for San Antonio Homes (2026)

Reduce your SAWS bill and qualify for rebates with these upgrades during your next remodel.

San Antonio consistently ranks among the fastest-growing cities in Texas — and water conservation is a real concern. SAWS (San Antonio Water System) actively promotes water-efficient upgrades, and some fixtures qualify for rebates. Here's what to consider during your next remodel.

WaterSense-Certified Toilets

Standard toilets use 1.6 gallons per flush. WaterSense-certified toilets use 1.28 gpf or less — saving up to 20% on toilet water use. During a bathroom remodel, upgrading to a comfort-height WaterSense toilet adds minimal cost ($200–$500 for the fixture) and can qualify for SAWS rebates.

Low-Flow Showerheads

Standard showerheads use 2.5 gallons per minute. WaterSense showerheads use 2.0 gpm or less. Modern low-flow showerheads maintain strong pressure through air-infusion technology — you won't notice the difference in feel. Cost: $50–$300 for the fixture.

Touchless and Motion-Sensor Faucets

Kitchen and bathroom faucets with motion sensors reduce water waste significantly — especially in households with kids. These fixtures run $150–$600 and are increasingly popular in mid-range to high-end San Antonio remodels.

SAWS Rebates — What's Available

SAWS offers rebates on qualifying water-efficient fixtures. Check saws.org/rebates for current programs. Common rebates include:

  • High-efficiency toilets: up to $100 per toilet
  • WaterSense showerheads: varies
  • Irrigation controllers: up to $200

Note: Rebate programs change. Always verify current availability at saws.org before purchasing.
