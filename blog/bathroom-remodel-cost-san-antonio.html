What Affects the Price Most

1. Tile Selection

Tile is the single biggest variable in any bathroom remodel. Basic ceramic tile runs $2–$5/sq ft. Porcelain and large-format tiles run $6–$15/sq ft. Custom or imported tile can hit $20+. Labor for intricate patterns adds another $4–$8/sq ft.

2. Plumbing Changes

If you're keeping fixtures in the same location, costs stay lower. Moving plumbing — adding a second sink, changing shower location, or relocating a toilet — adds $1,500–$4,000 depending on scope.

3. Shower vs. Tub

Converting a tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most popular upgrades in San Antonio. This project typically costs $4,000–$10,000 depending on the size and tile work involved. It also adds value in most San Antonio neighborhoods.

4. Bathroom Size

A guest bathroom (40–50 sq ft) costs significantly less than a master bath (80–120 sq ft). Larger bathrooms mean more tile, more labor, and more materials across the board.

Cost by Neighborhood in San Antonio

Labor costs are relatively consistent across San Antonio, but material choices and scope tend to differ by area:

  • Stone Oak / Shavano Park / The Dominion — Homeowners in these areas typically invest $18,000–$40,000+ in master bath remodels, focusing on custom tile, premium fixtures, and frameless glass enclosures.
  • Helotes / Boerne — Mid-range to high-end projects, $10,000–$30,000, with an emphasis on walk-in showers and double vanities.
  • North Central / Northwest SA — Range is widest here: $5,000–$20,000, depending on the home's age and the homeowner's goals.
  • Converse / Universal City / Schertz — Most projects in the $5,000–$15,000 range, focused on function upgrades and refreshes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in San Antonio TX?

In San Antonio TX, a basic bathroom remodel costs $4,000–$8,000. A mid-range remodel runs $8,000–$18,000. A high-end full renovation with custom tile, walk-in shower, and new vanity can range from $18,000–$35,000+.

How long does a bathroom remodel take in San Antonio?

Most bathroom remodels in San Antonio take 1–3 weeks. A basic cosmetic update (vanity, fixtures, paint) can be done in 3–5 days. A full gut-and-replace typically takes 2–3 weeks depending on tile work, plumbing, and permit requirements.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom remodel in San Antonio?

Permits are required when the remodel involves moving plumbing, electrical work, or structural changes. Cosmetic updates like replacing a vanity, toilet, or flooring typically do not require a permit. We handle all permits as part of the project.

What adds the most value to a bathroom remodel in San Antonio?

Walk-in showers, double vanities, and updated tile consistently offer the highest ROI in San Antonio. These upgrades appeal especially to buyers in higher-value neighborhoods like Stone Oak, Helotes, and Boerne.
