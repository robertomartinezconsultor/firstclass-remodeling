Services

Every room.
Every detail.

From kitchens and bathrooms to outdoor living and whole-home projects. Design consultation, project coordination, and bilingual support from start to finish.

Kitchen Remodeling

Custom cabinetry, quartz and granite countertops, islands, backsplash tile, lighting layouts, and full kitchen redesigns. We plan around how you actually cook, not just how it photographs.

  • Cabinet refacing vs full replacement guidance
  • Countertop material comparison (quartz, granite, marble, butcher block)
  • Layout optimization for workflow and storage
  • Appliance integration planning
  • Typical range: $20K – $80K depending on scope

Bathroom Remodeling

Walk-in showers, tub-to-shower conversions, double vanities, master bath redesigns. Waterproofing substrate and tighter grout lines are standard, not upgrades.

  • Schluter waterproofing systems
  • Tile selection: porcelain, ceramic, natural stone
  • ADA-accessible and aging-in-place options
  • Ventilation and moisture planning for TX humidity
  • Typical range: $8K – $40K depending on scope

Outdoor Kitchen & BBQ

Custom BBQ islands, built-in grills, outdoor bars, pergolas with fans, and covered cooking areas. Designed for San Antonio heat, rain, and year-round entertaining.

  • Gas line planning and grill integration
  • Shade structures: pergola, solid cover, retractable
  • Drainage and slope considerations for TX storms
  • Lighting for evening entertaining
  • Typical range: $18K – $60K depending on scope

Patios & Covered Porches

Covered patios, stamped concrete, tile work, pergolas. Extend your living space without compromising drainage, shade, or structural integrity.

  • Stamped concrete vs pavers vs tile
  • Foundation and slab assessment
  • Fan and lighting rough-in
  • Typical range: $12K – $40K depending on scope

Home Additions

New rooms, sunrooms, garage conversions, structural additions. Coordinated from design through completion so the new space feels original to the home, not bolted on.

  • Structural engineering coordination
  • Roofline matching and exterior continuity
  • HVAC and electrical extension planning
  • Typical range: $55K – $150K+ depending on scope

Flooring

Hardwood, luxury vinyl plank, large-format porcelain, natural stone. Subfloor condition checked before anything gets laid. TX humidity factored into every material choice.

  • LVP vs hardwood vs tile comparison
  • Subfloor leveling and moisture testing
  • Transition planning between rooms
  • Typical range: $5 – $16/sqft installed

Painting

Interior and exterior. Proper prep is where paint jobs live or die. Surface repair, priming, and finish coats with premium paint lines (Sherwin-Williams, Behr Pro).

  • Color consultation with Sherwin-Williams palettes
  • Cabinet painting and refinishing
  • Exterior: stucco repair, power wash, prime, finish
  • Typical range: $2.5K – $8K full home

Whole-Home Remodel

When multiple rooms need attention at once. Phased planning so you can stay in the home during most of the work. One point of contact, one timeline, one scope document.

  • Phased project scheduling
  • Design continuity across rooms
  • Budget allocation by priority
  • Typical range: $85K – $250K+ depending on scope
