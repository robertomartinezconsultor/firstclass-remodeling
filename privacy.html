Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

First Class Remodeling TX ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect from visitors to firstclassremodelingtx.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide: name, email, phone, ZIP code, and project details submitted through our contact or estimate forms.
  • Usage data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referrer) and analytics from Google Analytics 4.
  • Cookies: we use first-party cookies for language preference and analytics; no third-party advertising cookies are set without consent.

How we use it

  • To respond to consultation requests and send project estimates.
  • To improve the website, diagnose issues, and understand which content is useful.
  • To send follow-up communications only when you request them (we do not sell or rent contact lists).

Who we share with

We share limited data only with service providers that help us operate the site or communicate with you: Google Analytics, Formspree (form delivery), and Meta/Facebook Messenger when you initiate a conversation. None of these providers receive more than what is required to deliver their service.

Your rights

Texas residents, as well as California (CCPA/CPRA) and EU (GDPR) visitors, may:

  • Request a copy of personal information we hold.
  • Request correction or deletion.
  • Opt out of any non-essential tracking.

Send requests to estimates@firstclassremodelingtx.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Data retention

Lead form submissions are retained for up to 24 months for project follow-up. Analytics data is retained according to Google's default (14 months). You may request earlier deletion at any time.

Security

Submissions travel over HTTPS. We limit internal access to personal data to the owners of First Class Remodeling TX.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
