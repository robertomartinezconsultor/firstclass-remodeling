About

The short version:
we care about the details.

First Class Remodeling TX started with a simple observation: most homeowners in San Antonio have had at least one bad experience with a remodel. Timelines slip. Communication drops. The final result looks different from what was discussed. We set out to fix that.

For over 15 years, we have coordinated kitchen, bathroom, outdoor, and whole-home remodels across San Antonio and surrounding areas. Every project starts with a free design consultation at your home. We walk the space, talk through the vision, and follow up with a detailed written plan.

What makes the difference is not what we promise. It is what we check. Waterproofing substrate before tile goes down. Subfloor condition before flooring gets laid. Drainage slope before a patio cover goes up. The things you do not see are the things that make a remodel last.

We are bilingual. We coordinate every project from design to completion. And we answer the phone when you call.

Written scope, always

Every project gets a detailed written plan before work begins. No ambiguity. No surprises on the invoice. What was agreed is what gets delivered.

Bilingual from day one

Consultations, plans, updates, and project coordination available in English and Spanish. Your family should feel comfortable throughout the process.

Design to completion

One point of contact from first sketch to final walkthrough. We coordinate materials, scheduling, and quality so nothing falls between trades.
