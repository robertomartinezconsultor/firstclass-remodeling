Presentation is doing more work than it did a year ago.

Inventory in Sarasota and Manatee has tightened, and well-prepared single-family listings are moving. Condominiums are not moving the same way. The gap between the two is presentation as much as price.

A property that needs work before it shows well puts you in an awkward position: your client wants a number, and you need it before the conversation goes cold. That is the part we handle.

For more than 25 years we have coordinated renovations in San Antonio for fix-and-flip companies, Airbnb hosts, and investors, working alongside real estate agents on every one of those properties. We are bringing that same partnership to Sarasota, Bradenton, and Venice.
