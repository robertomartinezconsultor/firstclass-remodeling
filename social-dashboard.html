Auto-Post Your Real Photos

  1. Go to your GitHub repo
  2. Navigate to the analizar/ folder
  3. Click Add file → Upload files
  4. Drag & drop your remodeling photo
  5. Commit the file — GitHub Action runs automatically
  6. Claude Vision analyzes the photo and writes a caption
  7. Photo moves to mis-fotos/ and is added to the queue
  8. Your photo posts in the next scheduled slot

What the Bot Does

  • Checks queue FIRST before regular posts
  • Uploads your real photo as the Facebook image
  • Uses the Claude-generated caption
  • Marks it processed after publishing
